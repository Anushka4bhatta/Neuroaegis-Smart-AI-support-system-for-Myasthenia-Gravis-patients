What is Myasthenia Gravis?

Myasthenia Gravis (MG) is a chronic autoimmune neuromuscular disorder that causes fluctuating muscle weakness, especially in the eyes, face, throat, and limbs.

Early detection of fatigue, slurred speech, and ptosis can help patients seek timely medical care and avoid crises.
